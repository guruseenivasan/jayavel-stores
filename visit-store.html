Yes, absolutely. We have a dedicated packing desk equipped with custom bubble encapsulation, thermo-cushioning, and sturdy wooden crating tailored for international air and ocean luggage allowances.

You can message our WhatsApp Seervarisai coordinator at +91 98941 98916 or fill in the form above. We will send you our detailed curated Tamil Wedding Seer catalog (with weights, tier options, and custom name engravings) before your visit so everything is prepared upon arrival.
