Three Generations of Preserving Tamil Metallurgy & Household Divinity

Founded in 1978 in the bustling lanes of East Masi Street, just steps away from the sanctum sanctorum of the Meenakshi Sundareswarar Temple, Jayavel Stores was born to safeguard authentic metalcraft.

While modern markets flooded with machine-stamped thin alloys, our founders remained steadfast with master sthapatis from Kumbakonam and Nachiarkoil. We ensure every Kuthu Vilakku is cast with solid bell metal, every Eeya Chombu holds genuine aromatic properties, and every pooja idol reflects timeless Vedic iconography.
